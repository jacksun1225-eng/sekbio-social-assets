An IGRA spends 16 to 24 hours in the incubator. The IFN-γ readout doesn't have to wait for an ELISA plate.

Our TB IFN-γ Rapid Test Uncut Sheet is a colloidal gold lateral flow master card that detects interferon-gamma in serum or plasma. You cut it into 4.0 mm strips, house it and label it as your own cassette.

From the instructions for use:
✅ 20 µL serum or plasma + 100 µL sample diluent
✅ Read at 15 minutes; do not interpret after 20
✅ Serum, or heparin, citrate or EDTA plasma
✅ Sheets keep 24 months sealed at 4–30°C

What it does not do: replace antigen stimulation. Nil, TB-antigen and mitogen tubes stay part of your design. The strip swaps out step 4 of the IGRA only.

Published lateral flow IGRA studies (QIAreach QFT, a reader-based product, not this sheet) found 76% to 96% agreement with ELISA, with disagreement clustered near the cutoff. That's where a visual strip needs testing hardest, so ask us for the detection limit, ELISA agreement and precision data before you design around it.
